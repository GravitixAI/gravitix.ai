# Gravitix AI — product brief

Living document for why this site exists, who it serves, and what we build next.
Update this as the idea gets sharper. The public site should follow this file, not the other way around.

## Why this exists

Gravitix AI is a learning home for people who want to understand how modern AI actually works — not just how to prompt it.

Serious AI education should be clear, structured, and usable.

Most AI education platforms assume the learner already has enough math, science, and tech. We will serve that audience too. We will also serve the people those platforms skip: young students who have not yet reached the school level where relevant courses appear, and the parents and educators who need to know when to step up and where to steer.

## Audiences

Who we are building for. Add, split, or drop rows as we learn.

| Audience | What they want | What we should not assume |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Young students (pre-AI-course age) | A clear, age-right path through the math, science, and tech they will need before formal AI study | They already have algebra, stats, programming, or “AI literacy” from school |
| Parents | When to raise the difficulty, what to introduce next, and how to steer without guessing | They know the school sequence or the AI landscape |
| Educators | A curriculum map they can use to pace and place students | Their school already offers AI-prep courses at the right time |
| Builders / adult learners | How models work, not only how to prompt | — this is the more common AI-education audience; we serve them too |

Notes:

- We serve two layers: **prep** (students / parents / educators before the usual AI courses exist) and **builders** (people who already have enough schooling).
- Most AI platforms skip the prep layer. Gravitix should not.
- Primary audience for the first Courses + Learning Paths work: **young students, with parents and educators as the guides**.
- Who we are *not* for (prep branch): people looking only for prompt tricks or a chatbot tutorial with no foundations.

## Learning paths

Guided sequences from fundamentals to applied AI. A path is ordered. A course can appear on more than one path.

| Path | Who it is for | Starts with | Ends with |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| AI-ready foundations (prep) | Students not yet offered relevant school courses; parents and educators pacing them | Early math / science / tech building blocks | Ready to start formal AI study |
| Builder path | Learners who already have satisfactory schooling | How models work | Applied AI |

The **AI-ready foundations** path is the first project for both Learning Paths and Courses. It should say *what* to learn, *in what order*, and *when to step up*.

## What is in scope

What this website should do.

- Teach how models work, not only how to use chat tools
- Courses, learning paths, and resources in one place
- A branch for students, parents, and educators that elaborates the math, science, and tech that best prepare someone for the future of AI
- Guidance on *when* to step up learning and *where* to steer a child or student
- Preparatory courses, not only courses for people who already have enough education
- Enrollment / onboarding (Get Started)
- Staff accounts and roles (Phase 0 — live)

## What is out of scope

What we are not trying to be, at least for now.

- Prompt-only or chatbot-tutorial products with no foundations
- A replacement for a full school district curriculum (we steer and prepare; we do not become the school)
